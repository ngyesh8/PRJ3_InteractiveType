Slice of Life Wishes

A bit of my culture — to wish you well through the hardships of daily life and as a reminder to cherish small moments.



The Cantonese diaspora is marked by an old yet colorful vernacular. To me, the language reflects a key part of my distinctive culture, childhood, and relationship with my parents—all of which are fading as my community approaches a broader modern world where Mandarin has become the default rendition of the collective Chinese identity. The dominant culture that has arisen from Mandarin is nearly foreign to me. As a Yue Chinese descendant, I believe it is part of my responsibility to carry on Cantonese and its many semi-archaic traditions.

This project is also an ode to the Yue Chinese ancestors in San Francisco, who paved the way for many future generations and are now being forgotten by time.
